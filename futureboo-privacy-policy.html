Privacy Policy — Future Boo

Last updated: August 31, 2026

1. Introduction

The Future Boo app (the "App") uses artificial intelligence to generate a fun, fictional image of you with your future partner. It is published by its developer. Contact: ybouregba3@gmail.com.

This policy explains, simply and transparently, what the App does with your data. In short: your photo is processed for a few seconds, then deleted. Nothing else is collected.

2. Data processed

  • The photo you take or choose (your face): it is sent to our servers only to generate your personalized image.
  • Your quiz answers (preferred partner, style, vibe, zodiac sign…): used only to personalize the generation and stay on your phone.
  • Your remaining credits: stored locally on your device.

3. Your photo is deleted immediately

Your photo is processed in memory by our servers to generate the image, then permanently deleted right after (a few seconds). It is never written to disk, never stored, never sold, never used for any other purpose. The generated image is sent straight back to your phone.

4. What we do not do

  • No user account, no profile, no password.
  • No data collection: no personal data (name, email, location, contacts) is requested or collected.
  • No ads, no trackers (analytics), no cookies.
  • No data shared with third parties, except the AI provider (Google) that generates the image and also deletes data per its own policy.

5. In-app purchases

The App offers in-app purchases (packs of reveals) through Google Play Billing. Payments are fully handled by Google. The App never receives or stores your payment details. Google may share transaction information (purchased product, order ID) needed to deliver your purchase.

6. AI-simulated images

All generated images are fictional, AI-simulated, for entertainment purposes only. They do not depict any real person and are not a prediction. The App always displays this notice.

7. Storage and deletion

No data is retained on our servers. Your answers and credits are stored only on your phone: uninstalling the App deletes them.

8. Minors

The App is intended for people aged 13 and over.

9. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data. Since no data is retained, these rights are simple to exercise: delete the photo from your phone or uninstall the App. For any question, contact us at: ybouregba3@gmail.com.
